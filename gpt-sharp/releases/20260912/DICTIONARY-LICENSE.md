# SHARP challenge vocabulary

The bundled list contains 5127 entries of 3–7 ASCII letters and 769 six-letter racks, each with at least 12 accepted answers. It is a finite challenge list, not a complete English dictionary. Acceptance uses exact letter multiplicities and list membership. No runtime word-list fetch or legacy fallback is used.

Selection: common entries from first20hours/google-10000-english, commit d0736d492489198e4f9d650c7ab4143bc14c1e9e, google-10000-english-no-swears.txt; intersected with sindresorhus/word-list words.txt blob dd82aa6db5ac96e4a1950a6912f1fde2240e43db, with spelling-list checked supplements. Proper names/abbreviations excluded when absent from the spelling list. This automated check does not establish equal familiarity or difficulty; challenge validity and rack solvability are tested.

Source: https://github.com/sindresorhus/word-list
